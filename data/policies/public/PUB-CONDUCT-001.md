## Purpose

This document sets out the general rules, regulations, and code of conduct applicable to every individual working at Meridian Global Technologies Pvt. Ltd., regardless of department or role. It complements, and does not replace, department-specific policies (HR, Finance, IT) which employees should consult based on their role access.

## Core Values

The Company operates on four core values that guide decision-making at every level:

- **Integrity** — doing the right thing even when no one is watching.
- **Ownership** — treating Company outcomes as personal responsibility.
- **Respect** — valuing every colleague's contribution and perspective.
- **Transparency** — communicating openly, including sharing bad news early.

## General Conduct Expectations

All employees are expected to:

- Treat colleagues, clients, vendors, and visitors with courtesy and professionalism.
- Arrive prepared and punctual for meetings and commitments.
- Communicate constructively, even in disagreement.
- Respect shared workspace and Company property.
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the course of their work.

## Dress Code

The Company follows a business-casual dress code on regular working days. Client-facing roles or client meetings may require formal business attire, at the discretion of the reporting manager. Casual Fridays permit smart-casual wear, excluding beachwear, sleepwear, or clothing with offensive graphics/text.

## Workplace Safety

All office locations maintain fire safety equipment, marked emergency exits, and periodic evacuation drills. Employees must:

- Familiarize themselves with the evacuation plan for their floor/location.
- Report any safety hazard immediately to Facilities.
- Not prop open fire doors or obstruct emergency exits.
- Follow visitor sign-in procedures at all times for security purposes.

Any workplace injury, regardless of severity, must be reported to Facilities and HR within 24 hours.

## Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Employees must not offer, give, solicit, or accept any bribe, kickback, or improper payment in connection with Company business. Gifts from clients or vendors above a nominal value (INR 2,000) must be declined or, if declining would be culturally inappropriate, reported to Legal & Compliance and may need to be surrendered to the Company.

Facilitation payments of any kind to expedite government processes are strictly prohibited, even where locally common practice, in accordance with applicable anti-corruption law.

## Conflict of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation that could reasonably be seen as a conflict of interest, including:

- Financial interest in a vendor, competitor, or client.
- Close personal relationships with individuals in a reporting line (direct or indirect).
- Outside employment or board positions that could compete with or compromise Company interests.

Disclosures are made via the Annual Conflict of Interest Declaration or as situations arise, reviewed by Legal & Compliance.

## Social Media Policy

Employees are free to engage on social media in a personal capacity but must not:

- Share confidential or unreleased Company information.
- Speak on behalf of the Company without authorization from Corporate Communications.
- Post content that could be seen as harassing, discriminatory, or damaging to the Company's reputation.

Employees who identify as Meridian Global employees on public profiles are encouraged to include a standard disclaimer that views expressed are personal.

## Use of Company Assets

Company assets (devices, software licenses, physical equipment, intellectual property) are provided for business purposes. Employees are responsible for the reasonable care of assets issued to them and must report loss, theft, or damage immediately to Facilities/IT as applicable. All Company assets must be returned in working condition upon exit.

## Whistleblower Policy

Employees who become aware of illegal activity, fraud, or serious policy violations are encouraged to report it through the Ethics Line (accessible via the HR portal, available anonymously) or directly to Legal & Compliance. The Company prohibits retaliation against any employee who raises a concern in good faith, even if the concern is ultimately not substantiated after investigation.

All whistleblower reports are investigated by a team independent of the individuals or department involved, with findings reported to the Audit Committee where applicable.

## Disciplinary Framework Overview

Violations of this Code of Conduct are addressed through the disciplinary process defined in `PUB-HANDBOOK-001`, ranging from verbal warnings for minor first infractions to immediate termination for serious violations (fraud, harassment, safety violations, data theft). Legal & Compliance is involved in any matter with potential legal or regulatory implications.

## Policy Review

This document and all department-specific policies referenced within it are reviewed at least annually, or sooner if required by regulatory changes. Material changes are communicated to all employees via the HR portal and require re-acknowledgment.

## Contact

Questions about this Code of Conduct should be directed to `compliance@meridianglobal.example`. Concerns requiring confidentiality should use the Ethics Line described in Section 10.

## Purpose
This checklist walks a new employee through their first 30 days and gives managers a clear view of what should happen when. It complements the process described in the Recruitment Process & Hiring Funnel document, picking up exactly where that document leaves off (Day 1).

## Before Day 1 (HR Operations and Manager)
- IT asset request submitted (laptop, accounts) – see IT Runbooks, Laptop Provisioning SOP  
- Facilities/workspace allocated (or remote setup confirmed)  
- Buddy assigned by the manager  
- Day‑1 orientation scheduled by HR Operations  
- Welcome email sent with Day‑1 logistics  

## Day 1 Checklist (New Employee)
- Attend HR orientation session (covers benefits enrollment, HR portal walkthrough, Code of Conduct acknowledgment)  
- Collect laptop and complete IT setup (VPN, email, Slack, core tools per the Software & Asset Catalog)  
- Meet assigned buddy  
- Meet reporting manager for a 1:1 introduction and first‑week plan  
- Complete statutory documentation on the HR portal (PAN, bank details, prior employment documents)  

## Week 1 Checklist
- Complete mandatory Security Awareness Training (see IT Acceptable Use & Security Rules)  
- Read and acknowledge the Employee Handbook and Company Rules & Code of Conduct  
- Set up recurring 1:1s with manager  
- Get introduced to immediate team members  
- Review team‑specific onboarding materials (varies by department — Engineering, Sales, etc.; maintain their own onboarding guides referenced by the manager)  

## Weeks 2‑4 Checklist
- Complete any department‑specific systems training (e.g., CRM for Sales, codebase walkthrough for Engineering)  
- First goal‑setting conversation with manager, aligned to the Performance Management cycle described in the HR Employee Handbook  
- 30‑day check‑in with HR Business Partner  
- Benefits enrollment finalized (health insurance, if not completed on Day 1)  

## Manager Responsibilities During Onboarding
| Timing | Manager Action |
|--------|----------------|
| Before Day 1 | Assign buddy, prepare first‑week plan, request any team‑specific equipment |
| Day 1 | Welcome meeting, introduce to team |
| Week 1 | Daily check‑ins, ensure access/tools are working |
| Weeks 2‑4 | Set initial goals, begin regular 1:1 cadence, gather buddy feedback |
| End of Month 1 | Informal check‑in on how onboarding went, address any gaps |

## Buddy Role (What a Buddy Actually Does)
A buddy is a peer (not the manager) assigned to help a new hire navigate the informal, unwritten parts of the Company — where things are, who to ask about what, and general culture questions. Buddies are expected to check in with their assigned new hire at least twice in the first two weeks. This is separate from the manager relationship and is not a performance‑related role.

## Common Onboarding Issues and Who to Contact
| Issue | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Laptop or account not ready on Day 1 | IT Helpdesk (see IT Helpdesk & Team Structure document) |
| Benefits enrollment questions | HR Business Partner |
| Payroll/bank details not processing | HR Operations |
| Unclear on role expectations | Reporting manager; escalate to HR Business Partner if unresolved |

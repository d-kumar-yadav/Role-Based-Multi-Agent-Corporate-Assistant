## About Meridian Global Technologies

Meridian Global Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a B2B software company providing workflow automation and analytics tools to enterprise and mid-market clients across the technology, financial services, and retail sectors. Founded in 2016, the Company is headquartered in Bengaluru, with additional offices in Pune and Singapore, and a fully remote engineering pod supporting European client hours. As of the current fiscal year, the Company serves over 340 enterprise clients globally and employs approximately 410 people across all functions.

## Mission and Vision

**Mission:** To help enterprise teams eliminate manual, repetitive workflow bottlenecks through intelligent automation, so people can focus on higher-value work.

**Vision:** To be the default automation layer for enterprise operations teams worldwide.

## Organizational Structure

The Company is organized into the following major functions, each led by a function head reporting to the CEO:

| Function | Head | Core Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | CTO | Product development, infrastructure, security |
| Sales & Marketing | Chief Revenue Officer | New business, renewals, brand |
| Customer Success | VP Customer Success | Onboarding, retention, support |
| People & Culture (HR) | Head of HR | Talent, culture, employee experience |
| Finance & Admin | CFO | Financial planning, accounting, facilities |
| Legal & Compliance | General Counsel | Contracts, regulatory, ethics |

Each function operates its own sub-teams; organizational charts for individual departments are maintained on the internal People Directory and updated automatically as role changes are processed through HR.

## Leadership Principles

Decision-making across the Company is guided by the following principles, which complement the Core Values described in `PUB-CONDUCT-001`:

- **Bias toward action** — a reasonable decision made quickly is usually better than a perfect decision made slowly.
- **Data-informed, not data-paralyzed** — use data to support judgment, not replace it.
- **Default to transparency** — information is shared broadly within the Company unless there's a specific reason not to (e.g., compensation, active legal matters).
- **Customer proximity** — every function, not just Sales and Customer Success, is expected to understand client pain points directly.

## Internal Systems

Employees interact with several central internal systems, each owned by a specific function:

- **HR Portal** — leave, attendance, payroll, onboarding (owned by HR)
- **Finance Portal** — expense claims, budget tracking (owned by Finance)
- **IT Portal** — asset requests, ticketing, access requests (owned by IT)
- **People Directory** — org charts, employee search, reporting lines (owned by Corporate Communications, data sourced from HR)

Access to each system's underlying data is governed by the respective department's access policy — for example, department-specific salary or budget data is not visible company-wide even though the systems themselves are broadly accessible for self-service tasks like submitting a leave request or expense claim.

## Company Calendar and Key Events

- **All-hands meeting** — first Friday of every month; leadership shares business updates.
- **Quarterly town hall** — includes a live Q&A session.
- **Annual offsite** — typically held in Q1; location communicated by Corporate Communications each year.

## External Communications

Only designated spokespeople (CEO, function heads, or individuals explicitly authorized by Corporate Communications) may speak to media, analysts, or on public panels on behalf of the Company. Employees approached by media or press regarding Company matters should redirect the inquiry to `comms@meridianglobal.example` rather than responding directly, consistent with the Social Media Policy in `PUB-CONDUCT-001`.

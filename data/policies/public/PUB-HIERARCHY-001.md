## Purpose

This document describes how the Company is structured from a reporting and level perspective, complementing `PUB-ORG-001`. It answers the practical "who reports to whom" and "what does each level mean" questions employees and new hires frequently have.

## Career Levels

The Company uses a consistent level structure across all functions, so that "Senior" or "Lead" means roughly the same seniority whether in Engineering, Sales, or HR:

| Level | Typical Titles | Typical Experience |
|---|---|---|
| L1 — Associate | Associate, Analyst | 0-2 years |
| L2 — Mid-level | Engineer, Specialist, Executive | 2-5 years |
| L3 — Senior | Senior Engineer, Senior Specialist | 5-8 years |
| L4 — Lead/Manager | Lead, Manager | 8-12 years |
| L5 — Principal/Director | Principal, Director | 12+ years |
| L6 — Executive | VP, Chief Officer | Leadership track |

Promotion between levels follows the Performance Management cycle described in `PUB-HANDBOOK-001`, requiring two consecutive strong ratings plus Department Head sign-off for L4 and above. Compensation bands per level are defined in `HR-COMP-001`.

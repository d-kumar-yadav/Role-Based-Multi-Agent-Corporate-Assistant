## Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the standard process for identifying, evaluating, and hiring candidates for all open positions at Meridian Global Technologies Pvt. Ltd. It applies to all full‑time, contract, and intern hiring across departments and locations.

## Manpower Planning
All hiring requisitions must originate from an approved headcount plan. Hiring Managers submit a Position Requisition Form via the ATS (Applicant Tracking System), including:

- Business justification (backfill vs. new headcount)  
- Budget approval reference number  
- Required skills and experience level  
- Target compensation band  

Requisitions above the approved annual headcount plan require additional sign‑off from the Department Head and Finance.

## Job Posting Guidelines
Job postings must be reviewed by HR before publishing to ensure compliance with equal opportunity language requirements and consistency with the approved compensation band. Postings should avoid:

- Age, gender, or marital status preferences  
- Unnecessarily restrictive degree requirements not tied to the actual role  
- Internal jargon that obscures the actual responsibilities  

Postings remain live for a minimum of 7 working days before a candidate can be moved to offer stage, except for referral‑sourced candidates.

## Sourcing Channels
Approved sourcing channels include the careers page, LinkedIn, approved recruitment agencies (see empaneled vendor list on the HR portal), employee referrals, and campus placement drives. Use of unapproved third‑party sourcing agencies requires prior Procurement and HR sign‑off.

| Channel          | Typical Use                     | Approval Needed          |
|------------------|---------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Careers page / LinkedIn | All roles                     | None                     |
| Employee referral| All roles                       | None                     |
| Recruitment agency| Senior/specialized roles        | HR + Procurement         |
| Campus drive     | Entry‑level/graduate roles      | HR + Department Head     |

## Interview Process
Standard interview process consists of the following stages, though the number of technical rounds may vary by role seniority:

1. Resume screening by HR/recruiter.  
2. Recruiter phone screen (30 minutes).  
3. Technical/functional round(s) (1‑3 rounds depending on level).  
4. Hiring Manager round.  
5. Culture/values round (typically with a cross‑functional panelist).  
6. Final decision and offer approval.  

All interview panelists must complete Interviewer Training before conducting panels, and must submit structured feedback in the ATS within 24 hours of each interview.

## Selection Criteria and Scorecards
Every open role must have a documented scorecard defining the must‑have and nice‑to‑have criteria before interviews begin. Panelists score candidates independently against the scorecard; scores are not shared between panelists until all interviews are complete, to reduce anchoring bias.

A candidate must receive a “hire” or “strong hire” recommendation from at least 75 % of panelists to proceed to offer stage.

## Background Verification
All offers are contingent on successful completion of background verification, covering:

- Identity and address verification  
- Employment history verification (previous 2 employers minimum)  
- Education verification  
- Criminal record check (where legally permissible)  
- Reference checks (minimum 2 professional references)  

Background verification is conducted by an approved third‑party vendor and must be completed before the candidate’s confirmed start date. Adverse findings are reviewed jointly by HR and Legal before any offer is rescinded.

## Offer and Negotiation Guidelines
Compensation offers must fall within the approved band for the role and level. Any exception requires written approval from the Department Head and Head of HR.

Offer letters are generated and issued exclusively by HR — hiring managers must not verbally commit to specific compensation figures during interviews.

## Onboarding Checklist
Upon offer acceptance, HR initiates the onboarding workflow, which includes:

- Background verification kickoff  
- IT asset request (laptop, accounts, access provisioning)  
- Facilities/workspace allocation  
- Buddy assignment  
- Day‑1 orientation scheduling  
- Statutory documentation collection (PAN, bank details, prior employment documents)  

New hires are expected to complete all onboarding portal tasks within their first 5 working days.

## Referral Program
Employees who refer a successfully hired candidate are eligible for a referral bonus, payable after the referred candidate completes 90 days of employment. Referral bonus amounts vary by role level and are published on the HR portal.

Employees may not refer immediate family members for roles within their own reporting line.

## Diversity and Inclusion in Hiring
Hiring panels for roles above a specified seniority level must include at least one panelist from a different function to reduce homogeneity in evaluation. Recruiters are expected to actively source from diverse candidate pools where feasible, in accordance with the Company’s broader D&I commitments.

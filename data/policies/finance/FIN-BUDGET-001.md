## Purpose

This report summarizes departmental budget allocation, actual spend, and variance for Q3 FY2025-26 (October‑December), for use in the quarterly business review and departmental planning discussions.

## Company-Wide Financial Summary

| Metric                 | Q3 Actual          | Q3 Budget          | Variance |
|------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|----------|
| Total Revenue          | INR 42.3 crore     | INR 39.8 crore     | +6.3%    |
| Total Operating Expense| INR 26.1 crore     | INR 27.4 crore     | -4.7%    |
| Gross Margin           | 61.0%              | 58.5%              | +2.5 pts |
| EBITDA                 | INR 11.8 crore     | INR 9.6 crore      | +22.9%   |

Revenue outperformance was primarily driven by higher‑than‑forecast renewal rates in the enterprise segment (94% vs. 88% forecast) and one large mid‑market deal closing ahead of schedule.

## Departmental Budget Breakdown

| Department          | Q3 Budget (INR lakh) | Q3 Actual (INR lakh) | Variance |
|---------------------|----------------------|----------------------|----------|
| Engineering         | 850                  | 812                  | -4.5%    |
| Sales & Marketing   | 620                  | 668                  | +7.7%    |
| HR & People         | 180                  | 165                  | -8.3%    |
| Finance & Admin     | 140                  | 138                  | -1.4%    |
| IT Infrastructure   | 210                  | 245                  | +16.7%   |
| Customer Success    | 240                  | 232                  | -3.3%    |

**Notable variance — IT Infrastructure (+16.7%)**: driven by an unplanned cloud infrastructure scaling cost following the enterprise client onboarding in November, and an accelerated laptop refresh cycle. Finance has flagged this for review in the Q4 planning cycle; see FIN-2025-015 (Software Spend Freeze) issued as a related control measure.

**Notable variance — Sales & Marketing (+7.7%)**: driven by increased event and travel spend supporting the enterprise segment push, offset partially by lower‑than‑planned digital ad spend.

## Revenue by Segment

| Segment      | Q3 Revenue (INR crore) | % of Total | YoY Growth |
|--------------|------------------------|------------|------------|
| Enterprise   | 24.6                   | 58.2%      | +34%       |
| Mid‑Market   | 12.8                   | 30.3%      | +18%       |
| SMB          | 4.9                    | 11.5%      | -6%        |

The SMB segment decline is consistent with the planned strategic de‑emphasis of this segment communicated in the FY2025-26 annual plan, as resources are reallocated toward Enterprise and Mid‑Market.

The Company remains debt‑free with no external financing activity during the quarter.

## Headcount and Attrition

Total headcount closed the quarter at 412, against a budgeted plan of 425, primarily due to slower‑than‑planned hiring in Engineering (8 open positions carried into Q4) and two unplanned attritions in the Customer Success team. Voluntary attrition for the quarter was 2.9%, within the target range of under 4% quarterly.

## Q4 Forecast and Planning Notes

Based on Q3 performance, FP&A has revised the full‑year revenue forecast upward from INR 158 crore to INR 164 crore. Department Heads should submit Q4 reforecasts by the 10th of the month following any quarter where actual spend deviates from budget by more than 10%, per the Finance Policies document (Section 8, Budget Allocation and Variance Reporting).

The IT Infrastructure budget variance flagged in Section 3 will be reviewed as part of the Q4 vendor consolidation initiative referenced in FIN-2025-015.

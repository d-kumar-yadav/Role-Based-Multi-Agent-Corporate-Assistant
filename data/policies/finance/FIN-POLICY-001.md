## Purpose and Scope

This document sets out the Company’s expense reimbursement policy, travel policy, approval matrices, and a log of recent internal finance memos. It applies to all employees who incur business expenses on behalf of the Company.

## Expense Categories

| Category                | Examples                                 | Requires Pre‑Approval                     |
|-------------------------|------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Travel                  | Flights, trains, cabs                    | Yes, above INR 15,000                     |
| Accommodation           | Hotels during business travel            | Yes                                       |
| Client entertainment    | Meals with clients/partners              | Yes, above INR 5,000                      |
| Office supplies         | Stationery, small equipment              | No, up to INR 3,000/month                 |
| Software subscriptions  | SaaS tools for team use                  | Yes, all amounts                          |
| Training and certification | Courses, conferences                 | Yes                                       |
| Internet/phone (Remote work connectivity) | —                     | No, within monthly cap                    |

## Reimbursement Process

1. Employee submits expense claim via the Finance portal within 15 days of incurring the expense, with itemized receipts attached.  
2. Claims are auto‑routed to the reporting manager for approval based on the Approval Matrix (Section 5).  
3. Finance reviews for policy compliance and processes payment within 10 working days of manager approval.  
4. Reimbursements are disbursed along with the next payroll cycle or via direct transfer for urgent cases.  

Claims submitted more than 60 days after the expense date require additional Finance Head approval and a written justification.

## Travel and Per Diem Policy

| Travel Type | Domestic Per Diem (INR) | International Per Diem (USD) |
|-------------|------------------------|------------------------------|
| Employee (below Economy) | 1,500 /day | 60 /day |
| Economy (Premium Manager to Director) | 2,000 /day | 80 /day |
| Business (VP and above) | 2,500 /day | 100 /day |

Per diem covers meals and incidental expenses and does not require individual receipts, but is subject to submission of a travel itinerary.

## Corporate Card Usage

Corporate cards are issued to employees at Manager level and above who travel frequently, subject to Finance Head approval. Cardholders must submit itemized reconciliation within 7 days of each statement cycle. Personal use of the corporate card is strictly prohibited and will be treated as a policy violation.

## Vendor Payment Policy

All vendor payments require a Purchase Order (PO) raised prior to service commencement, except for pre‑approved recurring vendors. Standard payment terms are Net 30 from invoice receipt, unless otherwise negotiated and documented in the vendor contract. Payments above INR 500,000 require two‑level Finance sign‑off plus Procurement verification of deliverables.

## Budget Allocation and Variance Reporting

Department budgets are allocated annually during the planning cycle (typically November–December for the following fiscal year) and reviewed quarterly. Department Heads are required to submit a variance report if actual spend deviates from budget by more than 10 % in any quarter, along with a remediation or reforecast plan.

## Petty Cash Handling

Each office location maintains a petty cash float not exceeding INR 25,000, managed by the designated office administrator. All petty cash disbursements require a signed voucher and receipt, reconciled weekly and audited monthly by the Finance team.

## Audit and Compliance

Finance conducts quarterly internal audits of expense claims, vendor payments, and petty cash records. External statutory audit is conducted annually. Any employee found to have submitted a fraudulent expense claim is subject to disciplinary action up to termination, and recovery of the disbursed amount.

## Purpose and Scope
This policy governs equipment provisioning, reimbursement, and asset management for employees working in hybrid or remote arrangements. It is jointly maintained by IT (equipment specification, provisioning, security) and Finance (reimbursement limits, approval, expense processing). The policy is cross‑referenced from both the IT and Finance policy sets because it does not fit cleanly under either function alone.

## Equipment Provided by the Company
| Equipment | Provided By Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop | Yes | Per Software & Asset Catalog spec for role |
| External monitor | Yes, for Engineering/Design roles | On request for other roles, Manager approval |
| Docking station | Yes, for Engineering/Design roles | On request for other roles |
| Headset | Yes | Standard issue for all remote employees |
| Ergonomic chair | No | Not shipped by IT; reimbursed, see Section 3 |

Equipment provided directly by IT follows the Asset Lifecycle rules in the Software & Asset Catalog document and remains Company property, subject to return upon exit.

## Home Office Reimbursement (Finance‑Administered)
Employees in approved remote arrangements (per the Work From Home Policy in the Leave & Attendance Policy document) may claim reimbursement for home office setup, subject to the following annual caps:

| Item | Annual Cap (INR) | Pre‑Approval Required |
|---|---|---|
| Ergonomic chair | 12,000 | No, within cap |
| Desk | 8,000 | No, within cap |
| Internet connectivity | 1,500 / month | No, within cap |
| Additional monitor (non‑Engineering/Design) | 10,000 | Yes, Manager approval |
| Minor accessories (mouse, keyboard, webcam) | 3,000 | No, within cap |

Claims are submitted through the Finance portal following the standard Reimbursement Process described in the Finance Policies & Expense Memos document. For any item over INR 5,000, an IT‑issued equipment compatibility confirmation is required before submission to ensure compliance with Company security and connectivity requirements.

## Why This Spans Two Departments
This policy is jointly owned rather than residing under a single department:

* **IT’s stake:** Ensuring any reimbursed equipment (e.g., a monitor, a router) meets security and connectivity standards, and that Company‑provided equipment is tracked in the Asset Management system regardless of who requested it.  
* **Finance’s stake:** Keeping reimbursement amounts within approved caps, processing claims through standard expense controls, and categorizing expenses correctly for budget reporting.

Employees should direct equipment specification and compatibility questions to IT, and reimbursement status and claim questions to Finance. Queries that involve both aspects (e.g., “Can I get a bigger monitor and how much will I be reimbursed?”) are handled by IT confirming compatibility first, then Finance processing the claim, as described in Section 3.

## Security Requirements for Reimbursed Equipment
Any reimbursed equipment that connects to the Company network (routers, docking stations with network passthrough) must meet the minimum security standards published by IT, including current firmware and WPA3 support for routers. Non‑compliant equipment already purchased is not eligible for reimbursement; employees are encouraged to check the approved equipment list on the IT portal before purchasing.

## Asset Tracking for Reimbursed Equipment
Reimbursed equipment purchased by the employee remains the employee’s personal property, is **not** tracked in the Company Asset Management system, and is not required to be returned upon exit. This contrasts with directly provisioned equipment, which remains Company property throughout its lifecycle.

## Approval Workflow
1. Employee checks the IT portal for equipment compatibility guidance (Section 5) if the item connects to the network or exceeds INR 5,000.  
2. Employee submits the expense claim via the Finance portal, referencing this policy.  
3. Finance validates the claim against the annual caps in Section 3.  
4. For items requiring manager approval (Section 3), the claim is routed to the employee’s manager before Finance processes payment.  
5. Reimbursement is processed per the standard Finance reimbursement timeline (10 working days from approval).
